# Publication review

Draft for review. Mel checks clinical wording, scope, claims and final public copy. Record who approved the version and when. Simon coordinates setup and measurement. Carry out publishing, sending or settings changes only with separate explicit user direction for that action.

Use public or synthetic material and aggregate counts. If a patient anecdote or identifiable details appear, omit them from the draft and request a generic teaching point. Never fabricate cases, outcomes, credentials or testimonials.

For each material health claim record the exact wording, a credible source, the population or context it applies to and Mel's review status. Hold unsupported claims and distinguish practitioner opinion and traditional use from research findings. Flag therapeutic claims, supplement promotion, testimonials and images for review against current applicable New Zealand requirements. Verify the official sources before giving compliance advice.

For email, check recorded consent by signup source, suppression handling and the unsubscribe route before a live send or import. Keep ambiguous contacts out of the send audience pending verification. Use clearly marked placeholders for missing dates, prices and URLs.
