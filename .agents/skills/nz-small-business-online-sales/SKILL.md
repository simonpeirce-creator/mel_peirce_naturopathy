name: nz-small-business-online-sales
description: Prepare New Zealand small-business checkout and operational checklists for products, digital downloads, webinars and training, including fulfilment, cancellations and refunds.
---

Confirm offer type, seller of record, sales territories, GST status, price, inclusions and delivery limits. Produce a checkout brief, delivery and support checklist, and test purchase plan. Identify responsibility for pricing, confirmation email, access, delivery, customer questions, refunds and reconciliation. For an event include NZ time zone, date, recording access, capacity, postponement and what happens if the organiser cancels. For physical products include shipping cost and timing, stock, returns and any product-specific obligations. Route GST or marketplace uncertainty to the [tax skill](../nz-sole-trader-tax/SKILL.md).

Verify [Commerce Commission online selling guidance](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/business/dealing-with-typical-situations/selling-goods-and-services/selling-online/) before advising on terms. Distinguish change-of-mind policies from statutory remedies. Flag unclear recurring charges, misleading price or availability claims and blanket no-refund wording. For health products or health-related copy apply the [publication review](../mel-business-context/references/review.md) and verify applicable product and advertising rules before a launch. Record open decisions. Prepare drafts and tests without placing orders, processing refunds or publishing.
