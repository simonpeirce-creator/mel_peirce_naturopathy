name: nz-practice-privacy
description: Review privacy administration for a New Zealand small health practice, including clinical and marketing records, access, retention, consent and incident triage.
---

Map each system by purpose, information held, owner, access, disclosures and retention. Separate clinical records, financial records and marketing lists. Use synthetic examples, anonymised categories and aggregate figures. Do not request identifiable patient records or contact exports. Identify whether the practice and activity are subject to the Health Information Privacy Code and whether health retention rules apply before proposing a schedule. Check ordinary business and tax retention separately. Identify platform hosting or access arrangements requiring review.

For marketing, record consent provenance and suppression handling and check unsubscribe functionality. Treat a clinical booking as distinct from marketing permission. For a suspected incident, lead with immediate containment steps appropriate to the system, such as revoking a public link. Preserve an internal incident record and route promptly to the privacy lead. Use [NotifyUs](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/privacy-breaches/notify-us/) to assess serious harm and verify prompt notification duties. Explain the distinction between a statutory obligation and OPC’s 72-hour expectation when relevant. For access or correction requests, assign an owner and verify the applicable response deadline. Return a practical checklist with evidence, decisions and follow-up owners.

Sources: [Health Information Privacy Code](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/codes-of-practice/health-information-privacy-code-2020/), [health retention regulations](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/1996/343/en/latest/sections/DLM225620/), [NZ spam law](https://www.dia.govt.nz/Spam-NZ-Spam-Law).
