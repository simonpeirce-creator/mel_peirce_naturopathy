name: nz-sole-trader-bookkeeping
description: Run a New Zealand sole trader's bookkeeping close, invoice and settlement reconciliation, debtor follow-up, cash forecast and accountant handover. Use for Mel's consultations and online sales.
---

Establish the period, GST status, accounting basis where relevant, payment channels and opening balances. Work from anonymised transaction rows, control totals and source references. Keep supporting documents in the accounting system.

Reconcile the bank to bookkeeping and each payment processor to its settlements. Bridge gross sales, refunds, chargebacks, fees, timing differences and bank deposits. Separate consultations, products, webinars and training. Prevent double counting of invoices and cash settlements. Separate owner drawings, capital introduced and internal transfers from business income and expenses. Leave ambiguous lines unresolved with an owner and evidence request.

Return a close summary with discrepancies, overdue invoices and next actions. For cash forecasts, show opening cash, expected receipts, committed outflows, confirmed tax and ACC due dates, and closing cash. Treat unpaid bookings, proposed launch revenue and owner drawings explicitly. Prepare accountant handover totals by category and list uncertain tax treatments for the [tax skill](../nz-sole-trader-tax/SKILL.md). Use [Business.govt.nz cash flow guidance](https://www.business.govt.nz/tax-and-money/business-finance-basics/cash-flow-forecasting) when building the forecast.
