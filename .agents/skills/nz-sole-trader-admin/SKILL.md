name: nz-sole-trader-admin
description: Set up and run a New Zealand sole trader's administrative calendar, registrations, ACC levy reviews, insurance renewals, supplier records and first-hire preparation.
---

Confirm structure, trading details, tax agent, accounting setup, GST status, ACC cover and levy classification, insurance dates and whether anyone is hired. For Mel, use the [practice profile](../mel-business-context/references/profile.md) for public context only. Do not infer a registration or policy from it.

Create a register with item, status, owner, next action, due date, source and evidence location. Use myIR, ACC invoices, policies and the accountant for exact dates. Add monthly reconciliation and a rolling GST-turnover check. Confirm business contact details, optional NZBN if applicable, invoice numbering, payment terms, recurring software, supplier agreements and insurance scope for consultations, products and events.

For an ACC review compare the invoice period, earnings basis, activity classification, cover and due date. Flag differences for ACC rather than guessing the levy. If hiring, establish whether the relationship is employment or contracting before setting up payroll or withholding. Verify employer registration, PAYE, payday filing, KiwiSaver and any relevant schedular payment obligations for the actual arrangement. Record decisions and referral questions.

Sources: [Starting as a sole trader](https://www.business.govt.nz/business-stage-or-type/sole-traders/becoming-a-sole-trader), [ACC CoverPlus invoice](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/received-an-invoice/explaining-your-invoice/coverplus-invoice), [IRD employers](https://www.ird.govt.nz/roles/employers), [Schedular payments](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/schedular-payments/making-schedular-payments/tax-schedular-payments-and-file-employment-information).
