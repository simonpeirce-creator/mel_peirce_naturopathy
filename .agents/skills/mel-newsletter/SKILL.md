Read the shared [Business profile](../mel-business-context/references/profile.md) and [Publication review](../mel-business-context/references/review.md). Ask for the topic, approved source material, reader takeaway and live or proposed destination when needed. Use a practical first-person voice, short paragraphs and a clear action. Provide subject, preview text, body, button text and destination, plus a separate claim and approval note.

For a welcome sequence, start with delivering the promised resource and setting expectations. Follow with one practical idea and a simple question about what the reader wants to learn. Introduce a waitlist or paid offer once it exists. Check what already runs in Flodesk before suggesting activation to avoid a duplicate welcome. Keep offer emails specific about what buyers receive. Leave unconfirmed dates, prices and URLs as placeholders.
