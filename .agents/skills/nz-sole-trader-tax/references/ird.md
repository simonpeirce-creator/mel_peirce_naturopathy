# New Zealand sole trader tax decisions

Source baseline reviewed 12 November 2026. Confirm rules, rates and dates for the transaction and income year. Mel's registrations, elections, tax agent, turnover and liabilities are open.

| Question | How to work it through | Official sources |
|---|---|---|
| Annual income tax | Reconcile business income and allowable expenses to net profit. Include other taxable income and relevant credits for an individual estimate. Owner drawings and capital introduced are transfers. Confirm IR3 and supporting business schedules, balance date and agent extension. Filing and payment dates are separate. | [Self-employed](https://www.ird.govt.nz/roles/self-employed), [IR3](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/individual-income-tax-return---ir3/complete-my-individual-income-tax-return---ir3), [filing date](https://www.ird.govt.nz/7-july) |
| GST registration | Check taxable turnover for the preceding and expected next 12 months across taxable activities carried on by the same person. Compare to the NZ$60,000-or-more threshold for either period and check whether the person already charges GST. Identify when the threshold was reached or expected. Confirm the effective date with IRD or the tax agent before changing invoices. Use turnover before expenses. Confirm the registration date, filing frequency and accounting basis. Do not add GST to prices or invoices based on an assumption that the business is registered. | [Registering for GST](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/registering-for-gst), [GST documentation](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/tax-invoices-for-gst) |
| GST on sales and refunds | Establish seller of record, customer location, supply type and NZ GST status before applying a rate. Check platform terms and IRD guidance for overseas webinar attendees or downloads. Reconcile refund adjustments and platform fees separately from gross sales. For confirmed standard-rated GST-inclusive NZD amounts, use the applicable GST fraction. | [IRD GST](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst) |
| Provisional and terminal tax | Check prior residual income tax rather than profit, turnover or the balance after provisional instalments. Prior RIT above NZ$5,000 generally triggers provisional tax. Check exceptions, method and dates in myIR or with the agent. Use forecast profit to anticipate cash pressure, especially in the first year. | [Provisional tax](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/provisional-tax), [interest and exceptions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/provisional-tax/interest-on-provisional-tax) |
| Expenses and assets | Identify business purpose and supporting evidence. Apportion mixed personal and business costs on a defensible basis. For home office and vehicles verify the selected method and records. Consider capital treatment, depreciation and Investment Boost eligibility before expensing equipment. Keep inventory and stock movements visible for physical product sales. | [Business expenses](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses), [Investment Boost](https://www.ird.govt.nz/investment-boost) |
| Records | Keep sales and purchase records for the applicable IRD period, generally seven years. Include processor settlements, invoices, receipts, refunds and evidence for apportionment. Handle clinical retention separately. | [IRD recordkeeping](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/record-keeping/records-of-income-and-expenses) |

For a reserve estimate, show the horizon, expected cash receipts, separate tax and ACC obligations and confirmed due dates. Avoid a flat reserve percentage presented as the user's actual liability. Ask for aggregate figures or anonymised entries.
