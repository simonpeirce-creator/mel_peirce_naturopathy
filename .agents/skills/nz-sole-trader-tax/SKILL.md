---
name: nz-sole-trader-tax
description: Answer NZ sole trader tax questions and prepare estimates or accountant handovers covering IR3, GST, provisional tax, deductions, assets and tax records. Use for Mel's practice and other NZ sole traders.
---

Read the [IRD decision guide](references/ird.md). Verify current official guidance for the relevant tax year and transaction date. Establish the tax year, balance date, GST status and relevant turnover or profit figures first. Only ask for figures that change the answer. For personal income tax estimates include other taxable income, credits and payments already made. Keep identifiers and source records in the user's accounting system.

State the likely treatment, the assumptions and the evidence needed. Where useful, show an NZD calculation with separate lines for GST, income tax and ACC, and say which figures are estimates. Use conditional outcomes where facts are missing. Escalate a specific unresolved classification, cross-border sale or material filing judgment to IRD or the accountant. Prepare a question and document list they can act on. Drafts and estimates do not authorise filing, payments or registration changes.
