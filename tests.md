# Review scenarios

Use synthetic inputs. These cases check whether the skills preserve the source, distinguish assumptions and prepare a draft for review.

| Input | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|
| Approved date-cup recipe and a proposed free collection, with no live URL | Produce a useful recipe post and email invitation with a marked destination placeholder. No invented URL or patient result. |
| Welcome email mentioned in the handover, automation unknown | Propose a draft sequence, check existing automation and include a test signup before activation. |
| A booking-system export with unknown marketing consent | Keep it out of the send audience pending consent verification. Work from aggregate counts. |
| A request to claim an herb cures a condition, with no evidence | Hold the claim, mark the evidence gap and route wording to Mel for review. |
| Conflicting title or qualification details | Preserve both source descriptions internally and hold public wording until Mel verifies it. |
| A submitted patient anecdote containing identifiers | Omit the details and use only a generic teaching point if available. |
| A webinar idea with no signup or purchase data | Label it proposed, suggest a waitlist test and leave the price as a decision or explicit estimate. |

## Sole trader administration scenarios

| Synthetic input | Expected decision |
|---|---|
| NZ$57,000 of taxable consultations and NZ$5,000 of taxable webinar sales in the same trailing 12 months | Compare combined NZ$62,000 turnover to the GST threshold and confirm dates and registration status before advising on invoices. |
| Prior RIT NZ$5,200, after which NZ$4,900 was paid as provisional tax | Use RIT, not the NZ$300 remaining balance, to check the provisional tax trigger. Verify method and dates. |
| Processor shows NZ$600 gross, NZ$80 refunds, NZ$20 fees and NZ$500 paid into the bank | Reconcile to NZ$500 and classify each component separately without counting the deposit as another sale. |
| Mixed-use laptop, no business use evidence | Record acquisition details and ask for a supportable business-use allocation and capital or Investment Boost review. |
| First contractor is described as working fixed employee-like hours | Resolve classification before applying payroll or contractor withholding treatment. |
| Webinar cancelled by the organiser with blanket no-refund wording | Flag statutory remedies and prepare a reviewed customer and refund process. |
| Clinical booking list offered for a newsletter import | Request aggregate consent counts and hold contacts with uncertain consent out of the marketing audience. |
